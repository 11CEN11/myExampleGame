Living Room
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two 
armchairs. Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along 
with two of your aunt's wretched attempts at oil painting. You 
can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good 
vacuuming. 
Doorways lead west to your father's office, north to your 
bedroom, and east to the kitchen. 
#
Office
This is your father's small but productive workspace with his desktop PC and bookcases 
surrounding the desk in the corner. 
A mountain of paperwork occupies the right most side of the desk each with things needed to be 
filled out for his company. 
The LED screen displays a email inbox numbering 20+ unread messages. 
You can return east to the living room. 
#
Kitchen
The large kitchen remains alight with two dimmed overhead lights over is center island. 
One sink sits on the island with a large number of dishes remaining unwashed inside. 
Another sink lays on the counter near the wall and the faint sound of a loose droplet hits the 
bottom of the silver bowl every few seconds. A dishwasher and oven/stove lay on either 
side of the wall sink. An ominous aura looms over the area of the kitchen. 
You can return west to the living room or you can travel south to the garage. 
#
Bedroom
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here 
when you were six. A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next 
to your twin bed. Posters of various anime characters cover one 
wall. A pile of laundry, slowly but surely attaining sentience, 
occupies the far corner. 
The door to the attic is in the ceiling above the pile of 
clothes. You can return south to the living room. 
#
Attic
The dark but large attic houses old decorations, hand-me-down clothes bins, and family picture 
books that feature you when you we younger. A single hanging light centered in the room 
makes the surroundings barely visible. The temperature becomes extreme up here depending on the season 
so you always feel uncomfortable here. You can return down to the bedroom. 
#
Garage
The garage features to large garage doors on the farthest side from the door you came in from. 
Furniture such as large metal tool cabinets, a large wood and metal workbench, and numerous construction 
appliances. You can return north to the kicthen.
